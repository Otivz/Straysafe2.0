Context Diagram (Level 0)

Figure __. StraySafe 2.0 Context Diagram (Level 0)

This text accompanies mermaid/00_context_diagram.mmd and the ten-process Level 1 set. The separate corrected/ folder is a nine-process logical alternative with a different data-store dictionary and should not be combined with these figures.

The context diagram shows StraySafe 2.0 as process 0. It exchanges information with five human entities: the Resident, Subdivision Leader, Barangay Staff, Administrator and Public Visitor. Five external service providers are also shown: Google Gemini AI, Cloudinary, Email Service, Web Push Service and OpenStreetMap Services. Internal processes and data stores are omitted at this level.

The Resident supplies account and pet data, reports and disputes, ownership claims, adoption evidence, messages and notification preferences. The system returns access results, pet profiles and QR cards, report and claim results, adoption records, messages and alerts.

The Subdivision Leader supplies credentials, scoped report and match decisions, pet registrations, permitted local custody and claim information, announcements and messages. The system returns access results, subdivision cases and pet records, match and claim reviews, rescue progress, permitted local holding records, messages and alerts. A leader's ability to update a local holding record does not extend to animals already held at a barangay facility.

Barangay Staff supply credentials, report and claim decisions, rescue assignments and updates, holding and care records, adoption decisions, alerts and messages. The Barangay Head Officer can also supply personnel account details. The system returns access and account results, escalated cases, assignments, claim and adoption records, holding records and messages.

The Administrator supplies credentials, account and record changes, AI engine configuration and rescan requests, system settings, landmarks and coverage information. The system returns access and action results, pet and report records, AI job status, dashboard statistics, maps, audit logs and notifications. AI engine configuration selects Gemini vision or attribute-only matching.

The Public Visitor does not need an account. A visitor can submit a QR token, contact details and the location where a pet was found, and receive authorized public pet information. A visitor can also submit a certificate number to verify an adoption certificate; the system returns its validity and public verification details.

Google Gemini AI receives photos and descriptions and returns suggested animal details and comparison results. Cloudinary receives uploaded photos, videos and documents and returns stored file links. Email Service receives OTP and status-email data. Web Push Service receives notification data. OpenStreetMap Services receives location coordinates or map extents and returns map tiles, street addresses and routes.
