## Intent

Remove what the approved StraySafe 2.0 scope does not use and nothing reads: the `report_verifications`, `pet_vaccinations` and `behavior_tags` tables, report comments, announcement comments and reactions, and pet QR tag generation, scanning, endpoints, tables and screens. Consumers first, then models, start-up code and the SQL snapshot.

## Key changes

- **Backend, removed:** the whole pet QR router (QR generation, the public scan page API, the finder form, recovery requests, scan history, and the pet history timeline endpoint that lived in it, which no screen called) with its model and schemas, and the QR email, notification and push mapping (`notify_qr_scanned`, `render_qr_scanned_email`). Report comments (`POST /reports/{id}/comments`, the `Comment` model, `Report.comments`, every eager load, `comments_open` and its visibility helpers). Announcement comments and reactions (`POST /announcements/{id}/comments` and `/react`, both models and schemas, `announcements.allow_comments`). `ReportVerification` and `PetVaccination` (models, relationships, the `move(PetVaccination ...)` line of the pet merge). `PetHistory.scan_id`. QR generation on pet registration and restore, and QR cleanup on pet delete. `ReportResponse` no longer has `comments`, `comments_open`, `pet_qr_code_url`, `pet_qr_code_hash` or `pet_qr_token`.
- **Start-up (`main.py`):** the `pet_qr` router and model imports, `ensure_qr_tables_exist`, `ensure_report_verifications_columns` and the `allow_comments` column step are gone; `ensure_pet_recovery_and_history_tables` became `ensure_pet_history_table`, which only creates `pet_history` (without `scan_id`). 55 `ensure_*` functions became 53. Nothing that was removed is recreated.
- **Frontend, removed:** the QR scanner button and modal from the four navbars; the landing page's "Scan Pet QR" button and QR feature card (the card now describes the optional pet registry); the QR tag buttons, lightbox and scan-history links in the pet screens; the lost-pet report's QR text and the "pet found / confirm retrieval" recovery banner; the QR cards in the resident and leader report views; the QR scan notification route; and the seven QR pages and modals (`GuestQRScannerModal`, `QRScannerModal`, `PetRecoveryModal`, `PetQrCardPage`, `PetScanHistoryPage`, `PetScanPage`, `PetScanSuccessPage`) with their routes. Report comments (the leader, admin and resident comment sections and handlers, the case summary's Comments section and `orderedComments`) and announcement likes and comments (resident feed, leader alerts page and its detail view). The AI photo check's "QR tag detected" field is kept: it describes what a photo shows, not the pet QR feature.
- **Dependencies:** removed `html5-qrcode` and `jsqr` from `frontend/package.json` and `frontend/package-lock.json` (edited by hand; their only importers were the deleted scanner modals; no other lockfile entry depended on them; `node_modules` was not touched). **Kept** `qrcode` and `types-qrcode` in `backend/requirements.txt`: `routes/adoptions.py` still imports `qrcode` at the top of the file to draw adoption certificate QR codes; they go with the adoption retirement (a comment in the file says so).
- **SQL snapshot:** `Database3.4_subdivision.sql` (and its byte-identical `.txt`) lost 8 tables (`report_verifications`, `pet_vaccinations`, `behavior_tags` with its seed rows, `comments`, `announcement_comments`, `announcement_reactions`, `pet_qr_codes`, `pet_qr_scans`) and the columns `announcements.allow_comments` and `pet_history.scan_id` with its key and foreign key: **80 to 72 tables**, with a dated revision note at the top.
- **Tests and scripts:** the QR, comment and reaction checks were removed from seven backend tests; the chat-thumbnail half of `test_qr_limit_and_chat_thumb.py` lives on as `test_chat_thumb.py`; the QR step of `run_admin_readiness_audit.py` and a stale import in `unlink_owner_rejected_matches.py` were removed; the two case-summary browser tests and their fixture no longer assert comments.
- **Docs:** `DATABASE_SCHEMA.md` (72 tables), `DATABASE_FILES_AND_MIGRATIONS.md` (new "Retired tables" section), `overview.md`, `docs/02-project/README.md` and `CUTOVER_READINESS.md` (the QR card is no longer a legacy-only function waiting for a decision).
