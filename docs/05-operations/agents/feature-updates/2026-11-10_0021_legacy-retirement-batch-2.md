## Intent

Finish removing what the approved Subdivision-only scope no longer uses, in the order the plan documented, without building a replacement rescue, shelter, adoption or fine-processing system: endorsement letters become referrals; lost/found matching, AI suggestions, owner confirmation and related-case links no longer depend on custody or claims; the adoption, holding, legacy rescue, legacy claim and fee subsystems go, with their routers, utilities, models, relationships, foreign keys and start-up migrations; and the canonical SQL snapshot matches the remaining models.

## Key changes

- **Letters to referrals:** `barangay_referrals` no longer writes or reads letters (`letter_title`, `letter_file_url` are gone); the leader's "endorse to Barangay" after a second warning creates or updates a `BarangayReferral`; "is this case escalated", chat access, case review, matching scope and owner follow-up details are decided by the referral log, the status and the history, never by a letter.
- **Matching decoupled:** `matches.py`, `manual_matching.py`, `case_groups.py` and `report_split` no longer touch claims or holding. The one rule that used a claim now reads the owner's answer: a resident cannot confirm further sightings of a merged case until they confirmed **the case's first report** (`initial_claim_completed` is true after that Yes; the response has no `claim` object any more). The old one-claim-per-case merge logic (`consolidate_case_claims`) is gone; the multi-animal split carries only the registered pet.
- **Owner return is the handover record:** `record_owner_return` no longer needs claim fees, holding or a claim; it records the return, updates the pet and its history, marks the case's reports Claimed by Owner and closes the case's chats. The resident's lookup of a return no longer falls back to a claim.
- **Other readers:** pets (no adoption lock or claim step in assigning an owner), staff pet-record editing (now: the pet is linked to an **open case** in the staff member's jurisdiction; the holding and adoption custody rules are gone), admin dashboard counts, landmarks (orphan re-link by Barangay custody only), warnings, case closure (an ended case still ends its owner observation and lost-pet alert), analytics (rescue, custody, holding, adoption and claim sections removed from the payload), the operations watcher (overdue-holding, monitoring and adoption jobs removed), push categories and deep links, notification and e-mail renderers.
- **Deleted backend code:** the routers `rescue`, `holding`, `claims`, `claim_assessments`, `adoptions`, `adoption_chat`, `adoption_tasks`, `adoption_certificates` and `sidebar`; the models `pet_claim`, `claim_assessment` and the classes `EndorsementLetter`, `LetterStatus`, `Rescue`, `RescueAssignment`, `RescueStatus`, `FacilityStatus`, `HoldingAnimal`, `HoldingTimeline` and the nine `Adoption*` classes in `models/report.py`; the schemas; the utilities `adoption_*`, `claim_*`, `holding_stay`, `id_security`, `pet_adoption`, `live_dispatch_tracker`. Shared helpers that live features used moved first (`utils/db_clock.py`, the lost-pet category helpers in `utils/case_vocab.py`).
- **Columns and relationships dropped from the models:** `report_media.holding_log_id`, `report_returns.holding_id`, `status_history.rescue_id` and `rescue_status_id`, the `Report.rescues` and `Report.endorsement_letter` relationships, the transient pickup fields; `chat_threads.thread_type` is `('Report','Direct')`. `reports.facility_id`, `custody_status` and `claimed_at` stay.
- **Start-up (`main.py`):** ten `ensure_*` functions, the adoption 9-stage migration call, the removed tables' indexes and every retired router are gone; nothing at start-up can recreate a removed table. The kept column migrations that sat inside the adoption function moved to `ensure_supporting_columns`, and the `chat_threads` lookup index to `ensure_performance_indexes`. `utils/retired.py` keeps the Barangay-account block and the landmark holding-place guard; `KEPT_WRITE_ROUTES` is empty.
- **SQL snapshot:** `Database3.4_subdivision.sql` and its byte-identical `.txt` lose 21 tables (72 to 51; a result, not a target) and the four columns above; a revision note explains it. Checked against the ORM: 51 tables, no snapshot-only or model-only table, no foreign key to a missing table; the three column differences left (`announcement_media.created_at`, `report_categories.description`, `subdivisions.boundary_polygon`) predate this work.
- **Frontend:** the Barangay staff pages, navbars and sidebar, the adoption pages, forms, certificate and agreement components, the claim-fee panels and the utilities only they used are deleted (46 files); old adoption addresses show the existing "no longer available" page through one wildcard route each; the Admin sidebar loses "Adoption Archive" and its badge, the Admin dashboard its adoption card and rescue/adoption rows, the Subdivision sidebar its claims badge call, the Admin heat map its rescue-request read, the analytics page its rescue, custody, holding and adoption cards, and the referral archive page its rescue-request and letter reading.
