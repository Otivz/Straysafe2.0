## Intent

Bring the approved prototype into the real Subdivision Leader app for Lost Pet and Found Animal reports, on top of the backend in [the backend record](2026-11-12_0226_lost-found-matching-backend.md): the Leader sees the possible matches the system found, reviews them in one place, and decides; a missing pet and a found animal are simply monitored from the start. No other category's screens change.

## Key changes

- **Data layer.** `adminApi` gets the match calls; `AdminState.matches` is read with every full load and again after a decision, a closure, a reopening or a recorded recovery; one new action `match/decide`; the comparison rows are read when the dialog opens (`matchDetail`). A notification about a possible match now opens the report it names.
- **Dashboard.** "Possible matches to review (n)" above "Needs your attention" (only when there is one), a line in the greeting, and "Missing and found animals" instead of "Missing pets". A missing pet or a found animal is no longer counted as a new report waiting for review (dashboard, attention list and the sidebar counter).
- **One review dialog** (`MatchCompareDialog`), opened from the dashboard or from either report: both reports side by side with the photo, the owner or finder and their own words; why they may match; a row-by-row comparison; then **Same animal: link them**, **Not the same animal** or **Decide later**. It says what a link does and does not do. A ruled-out match opens with **Review again**; a linked one is read-only. Double submission is guarded.
- **A missing-pet report and a found-animal report** show "Under monitoring" (`CaseStatusBadge`, also in the Cases list and the pet's related cases) with no Assessment tab, no status to move and no priority to set. Header actions: **Record contact**, **Add an update**, **Ask the Barangay** (a missing pet keeps **Record owner confirmation**). "Related reports" lists the matches waiting, the reports linked (with how the owner and the finder are getting in touch) and the ones ruled out; there is no Unlink for a link made from a match. A new found-animal overview (`FoundAnimalOverview`) mirrors the missing-pet page; the Barangay block is shared (`BarangayBlock`).
- **Kept on purpose:** a found animal keeps an **Ownership** tab (the ownership decision with evidence, and the claims residents can still make), because the claim flow depends on it. The generic "Link reports" is unchanged for the other categories.
- **Removed from these two kinds, as a consequence of "no assessment stages":** a missing pet's identity decision on the Assessment tab. The registered-pet panel no longer tells the Leader to "record your decision" there.
- **Ask the Barangay** for these two kinds is one predefined choice (six kinds of help) and an optional note, plus "already contacted? Yes / Not yet"; a referral that is only written down still says nobody was contacted.
- **A latent race fixed in the data provider.** Opening a case right after sign-in could show an empty body when the list refresh finished after the case's own read, because the list refresh merged the opened case from a snapshot taken before that read landed. It now merges inside the state update. The extra matches request made it likelier; the existing browser journey caught it.
- `Review match` buttons carry the names of the two animals for screen readers; the Ownership tab says so when the animal is wild.
