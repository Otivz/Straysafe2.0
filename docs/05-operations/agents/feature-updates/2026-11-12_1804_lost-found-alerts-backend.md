## Intent

A Subdivision Leader can send residents a notification alert about a missing pet or a found animal (now, at a chosen time, or repeating on a schedule that always ends) without it ever creating, republishing or editing the Community notice, and the alert goes out exactly once per planned time, through the in-app list and, once Firebase is set up, to Android phones.

## Key changes

- **Schema by a SQL file, not at start-up.** `backend/migrations/2026-11-12_lost_found_alerts.sql` adds `case_alerts`, `case_alert_sends` (unique per alert and planned time) and `user_notification_preferences.push_lost_found_alerts` (on by default); additive and safe to run twice. The two models carry `info={"migration": ...}`, `main.py` leaves them out of `create_all`, and `db_migrations/lost_found_alerts.require` (read-only) stops the start, naming the file, until they exist. The snapshot `Database3.4_subdivision.*` has them for new installs (59 tables, identical copies).
- **The service** (`services/case_alerts.py`): schedules (now, later, every N minutes/hours/days, set times of day, start now or later, end when resolved with a latest end of 14 days by default and at most 60, on a date, or after 1–500 sends), the 5-minute floor, edit (future sends only, never an immediate send except "Send now"), pause, resume without catch-up, stop and cancel; recipients (residents of the subdivision with the switch on, or for a missing pet the residents who reported a sighting; never the case's reporter or owner); one in-app row per alert per resident; the link a tap opens; staff lines in the case Activity; the title and message limited to 60/180 characters and checked like public notice text (no phone numbers, emails or exact addresses).
- **Exactly once:** each send claims its `case_alert_sends` row inside a savepoint; a second claim is refused by the database. After downtime only the latest missed send goes out (`skipped_before`). The Operations Watcher loop runs `run_due` every 60 s and re-checks that the case is open; closing a case stops every live alert (closure hook).
- **Routes** `/case-alerts` (Leader of the case's subdivision or the Administrator): list, audience (with `push_ready`), create (retry-safe `client_request_id`), update, pause, resume, stop; the case detail carries `alerts`; `GET /notifications/me` gives an alert its `link`.
- **Push:** FCM HTTP v1 through `google-auth` (no new Python package), on only when `FCM_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_FILE` names a file; the app's token is stored as an `fcm` device, moves to whoever registers it last, and is removed at sign-out; each delivery is claimed (`queued` → `sending`) before the network call, a stale claim is marked failed after 10 minutes; Android channel, tag and `data.url` in every message; category `alerts` follows `push_lost_found_alerts`. The Web Push public-key answer is unchanged.
