## Intent

The first version of the page (record 2026-11-12_0226-01) worked but failed the intended one-way workflow when looked at: a bright "Record owner confirmation" on a report that was only being monitored, four more competing actions in a row and a menu before the Leader understood the animal, four tabs, the status said four times, and eight stacked panels with the animal and what happened below the fold. The page was rebuilt around the Leader's actual job: see the animal, the owner or reporter and what happened; the report is "under monitoring"; branch only when something needs the Leader; go back to monitoring afterwards unless it is resolved.

## Key changes

- **One page, no tabs, no toolbar** (`MonitoredCasePage`, used by `CaseDetailPage` for a missing pet and a found animal only; every other kind of case keeps its workspace). From the top: **one status** ("Under monitoring", "Needs you · n", "Resolved", "Closed"); **the animal and what happened** with **who to talk to** beside it (photo, name, what it looks like, when and where it was last seen or found in the reporter's words, the owner or finder with tappable phone and e-mail); **one lane** that is either a quiet "Nothing needs you right now" with the situation in a line, or "Needs you", a short list with the one button that deals with each item (the strong colour only on the first); **only what exists** (sightings, linked reports, help from the Barangay, and for a found animal "Who does it belong to?" behind one line); **one activity list** with "Add an update" written in place; and **"Resolve this report"** behind one line.
- **What needs the Leader** is decided by pure functions (`data/monitored.ts`, `needsYou`), most pressing first: an owner and a neighbour asked for help; a neighbour may have the pet; a possible match; a claim; a follow-up due; a Barangay call only written down; a Barangay silent for three days. A call that was just made or just went unanswered is not asked about again at once, so a report returns to monitoring after the Leader deals with something. The possible-recovery record the system makes when a Leader links two reports is never listed as a sighting or lead.
- **Occasional actions sit beside what they act on**: "Log a contact" by the person, "Ask for help" by "Barangay not asked", "Create notice" by "No neighbour notice", the owner's confirmation and "Close without recovery" under "Resolve this report". Only the owner's confirmation records a recovery.
- **Two dialogs are plainer for these two kinds only**: "Record a contact" (how, when, who, what came of it, an outcome of Reached them / No answer / Something else) and "Ask for help" (one of six kinds of help; "Already contacted them?" now starts at "Not yet"; no repeated case rows). A match reads "This animal may be Coco" on the found animal's own page.
- **Removed** (replaced by the page above): the missing-pet and found-animal overviews, the related-reports and Barangay blocks of the first version (`LostPetOverview`, `FoundAnimalOverview`, `MatchesBlock`, `BarangayBlock`; the last three existed only in the earlier, uncommitted record), and the old tab, header-action and menu wiring for these two kinds.
- **A way to look at it with data**: `frontend/tests/demoLostFound.mjs` starts a throwaway copy of the app with a synthetic neighbourhood (a calm report, one with a match waiting, one with a help request and an unmade Barangay call, a found animal, a resolved report) and keeps it running until Ctrl+C. `docs/04-testing/evidence/lost-found-leader-page-2026-11-12/` holds before and after screenshots at desktop and phone width.
- **Consequences to approve**: a missing pet's identity decision (it lived on the Assessment tab) is no longer on the page; "Under monitoring" is what the screens say while a found report's server status stays `reported`; the Cases "Reported" filter still counts it.
- Documented in [LOST_FOUND_MATCHING.md](../../../01-system/LOST_FOUND_MATCHING.md) ("What the Leader sees", the decisions to confirm, the tests).
