## Intent

Correct the direction of the Subdivision Leader prototype. The first version (record `2026-11-11_1551`) added guided steps for lost pets, found animals, animal complaints and injured or dangerous animals, with waits, a derived "next step" and assessment-style dialogs. The developer asked for something much simpler and narrower: a Lost & Found Animal assistance and monitoring workflow where a report is under monitoring from the start, with no assessment stages, no manual status transitions and no unnecessary administrative steps.

## Key changes

- **Scope.** Only lost and found animal reports remain. Complaints, injured and dangerous animals and the whole "who has the move" model are gone. A test now fails if the preview mentions an incident category outside Lost & Found.
- **Workflow.** Every report is under monitoring on arrival. The Leader can contact the relevant resident, add an update, refer to the Barangay, and review a possible match. Two things only ever need a decision: a possible match, or a resident who asked for help.
- **Background matching, in both directions.** `matching.ts` compares a new report with the open reports of the other kind on kind of animal, microchip, colours, size, distinguishing features, collar, distance and time. Possible matches stay pending and tell nobody. Only the Leader's confirmation links two reports; it keeps both reports and histories, notifies both residents and shows the contact and coordination options, and it never verifies ownership or recovers anything. Several found reports can link to one lost report; a found report links to one lost report.
- **Same page whichever report comes first.** One report page (animal, people, assistance, related reports, history, and the actions) and one comparison dialog serve lost and found alike.
- **Barangay referral.** A predefined kind of help, optional notes, Yes or No for "already contacted or coordinated", Save. A referral saved as No is shown as "Not contacted yet", on the report and on the list, until it is marked contacted.
- **Recovery.** Unchanged principle: only the owner's confirmation. Simulated as the owner confirming in the app; the Leader can also record what the owner told them, as in the existing Leader screens.
- **Sample data and simulation.** 11 invented reports (lost and found, under monitoring and resolved), one possible match already waiting, confirmed links, and a Barangay referral written down but not made. The Preview menu delivers simulated reports from the Resident app (five, each once); the Preview panel on a report stands in for residents.
- Same boundaries as before: separate dev-only page, mock data, nothing sent or stored outside this browser tab (new `sessionStorage` key, so the earlier version's saved board is never loaded), nothing imported from the product or the backend, no change to the backend, database, Resident workflow, existing Leader screens or other incident categories, nothing committed or pushed.
