# Future Proposals

Use this area for ideas that have not entered current scope. A proposal is not a commitment until it is accepted through the project’s decision process.

| Documentation ID | Record | Purpose | Evidence question |
|---|---|---|---|
| DOC-06-01 | Problem statement | Keeps proposals grounded in a real need. | Is the affected user, problem, and supporting evidence clear? |
| DOC-06-02 | Proposed outcome | Describes value without prematurely fixing implementation. | Is the desired outcome measurable and distinct from a chosen solution? |
| DOC-06-03 | Options | Preserves alternatives and tradeoffs. | Are credible alternatives, including doing nothing, compared fairly? |
| DOC-06-04 | Dependencies | Exposes prerequisites and external decisions. | Are technical, organizational, data, and vendor dependencies named? |
| DOC-06-05 | Risks and cost | Prevents benefits from hiding lifecycle costs. | Are delivery, operational, security, migration, and maintenance costs considered? |
| DOC-06-06 | Decision state | Stops unapproved work from appearing committed. | Is the proposal clearly marked proposed, accepted, rejected, deferred, or superseded? |
| DOC-06-07 | Promotion path | Defines how an accepted idea becomes current scope. | Does acceptance identify the decision record, owner, milestone, and documents to update? |
