# Archive Governance

Use this area to retain useful history without allowing superseded material to compete with current documentation.

| Documentation ID | Record | Purpose | Evidence question |
|---|---|---|---|
| DOC-07-01 | Archive criteria | Makes retirement consistent. | Is material archived only when it is superseded, obsolete, or retained for traceability? |
| DOC-07-02 | Archive metadata | Explains why an old record remains. | Does each archived item identify archive date, reason, and responsible owner? |
| DOC-07-03 | Replacement links | Routes readers back to current authority. | Does superseded material link to its replacement when one exists? |
| DOC-07-04 | Search separation | Prevents stale guidance from dominating discovery. | Are archives clearly separated and labeled in navigation and search? |
| DOC-07-05 | Decision retention | Preserves context for consequential choices. | Are material decisions retained according to legal and project needs? |
| DOC-07-06 | Sensitive-data review | Prevents archives from becoming unmanaged exposure. | Are secrets, personal data, and unnecessary operational details removed or access-controlled? |
| DOC-07-07 | Periodic review | Avoids permanent accumulation without purpose. | Are retention obligations and deletion candidates reviewed on a defined cadence? |
